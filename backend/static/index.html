Citation Auditor

Paste anything an AI wrote about the law. We check whether the cases are real.

    How this works

    1. Does the case exist? A straight database lookup. No AI in this step at all — whether a case exists is a fact, not a judgement.
    2. Does it actually say that? We read the opinion and check whether it supports the claim made about it. A real case cited for something it never held is the failure that slips past people.
    3. Is it still good law? We scan later cases for signs this one was overruled. A warning signal, not a professional citator.

    We check our own work. Every quote is confirmed to appear word for word in the source opinion before it is shown. If it does not match, the finding is discarded rather than displayed.

    This tool verifies citations. It does not give legal advice.
